# Test RFQ prompt

This is a sample prompt. Its items are relevant and close to the lines in the sample quotations (`Quote1`–`Quote5`) in this folder, so you can try the whole flow yourself without writing your own request.

Paste it into **Generate an RFQ** to create an RFQ the sample quotations can be evaluated against. Then save it, open **Evaluate Quotations**, select it, and upload the five quotation files. `EXPECTED.md` lists what to expect, for checking by eye.

```
I want to order 2 centrifugal pumps for the cooling water circuit, 150 m of 4 core armoured power cable, a dozen hex bolts M16 and 24 nos hexagonal nuts to match, 6 pressure transmitters 0-25 bar, 10 ball valves 1 inch and 5 gate valves 150# flanged, 3 drums of lubricating oil, 40 kg welding electrodes, 2 rolls PTFE thread seal tape, 15-20 pipe clamps, 8 pairs of safety gloves, 12 safety helmets, gaskets for the heat exchanger flanges, 5 filters, 2 tonnes of sand for the blasting pot, 4 mechanical seals, 10 bundles of cable ties, and some paint thinner, around 20 litres.
```

`RFQ-2026-10-01-Key.pdf` is the reference RFQ this prompt is expected to produce.
